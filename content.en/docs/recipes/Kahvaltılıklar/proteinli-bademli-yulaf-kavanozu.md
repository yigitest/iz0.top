# Overnight Protein Almond Oats

> [!NOTE]
> **Recipe Summary**  
> Nutritious, grab-and-go overnight oats made with unsweetened almond milk, protein powder, and ground flaxseed. Yields 3 jars (3 x ~330 kcal).

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Rolled oats | 160 g (6 x 1/4 cup) | Approx. 53 g per jar |
| Protein powder | 64 g (3 x 1/4 cup) | Vanilla or preferred flavor; approx. 21 g per jar |
| Unsweetened almond milk | 337 g (6 x 1/4 cup) | Approx. 112 g per jar |
| Ground flaxseed | 21 g (3 x 1 tbsp) | Approx. 7 g per jar |

## Instructions

{{% steps %}}
1. ## Prepare Glass Jars
   Arrange 3 clean 350-500 ml glass jars (such as Bonne Maman or Mason jars) on the counter.

2. ## Portion Dry Ingredients
   Evenly distribute the rolled oats (~53 g per jar), protein powder (~21 g per jar), and ground flaxseed (1 tbsp / 7 g per jar) across all 3 jars.

3. ## Add Almond Milk and Stir
   Pour ~112 g of unsweetened almond milk into each jar. Using a spoon or fork, stir each jar thoroughly from the bottom up to ensure no dry protein pockets remain.

4. ## Seal and Refrigerate Overnight
   Tightly screw the lids onto the jars. Place them in the refrigerator for at least 4 hours or overnight so the oats soften into a creamy pudding. Enjoy chilled.
{{% /steps %}}

## Useful Notes

> [!TIP]
> **Batch Scaling**  
> For larger batches or bigger appetite jars, the ingredients can easily be scaled proportionally (e.g. 267 g oats, 107 g protein, 562 g almond milk, 35 g flaxseed).\n
