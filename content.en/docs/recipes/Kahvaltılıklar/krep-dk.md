# Crêpes (DK)

> [!NOTE]
> **Recipe Summary**  
> Classic thin breakfast crêpes made with flour, eggs, milk, and a pinch of salt, quickly cooked in a skillet.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Flour | 3/4 cup (100 g) | |
| Eggs | 2 | |
| Salt | 1 pinch | |
| Milk | 250 ml | See notes |
| Butter | As needed | For greasing the skillet |

## Instructions

{{% steps %}}
1. ## Prepare the Batter
   Add the flour (100 g), eggs (2), and a pinch of salt to a mixing bowl. Gradually whisk in the milk (250 ml) until completely smooth with no lumps.

2. ## Grease the Pan
   Heat a skillet (about 23 cm / 9 inches in diameter) over medium heat and melt a small amount of butter, swirling to coat the surface.

3. ## Cook the Crêpes
   Pour a small ladle of batter into the pan, swirling immediately in a circular motion so the batter coats the bottom thinly and evenly. Cook the first side for 1–2 minutes, then flip. Cook the second side for about 1 minute, then transfer to a serving plate.
{{% /steps %}}

## Useful Notes

> [!TIP]
> **Milk Volume Tip**  
> If using a standard 200 ml carton of milk, you can top it up with 50 ml of water to maintain the recipe's ideal liquid balance.

> [!NOTE]
> **Heat Control**  
> For even cooking without scorching, cook the crêpes over medium heat.
