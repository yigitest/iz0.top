# American Style Pancakes (Jamie Oliver)

> [!NOTE]
> **Recipe Summary**  
> Fluffy and tender American-style pancakes made by whipping egg whites separately into stiff peaks and gently folding them into the batter (Jamie Oliver).

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Eggs | 3 large | Separated (yolks and whites) |
| Flour | 115 g | |
| Baking powder | 1 tsp | |
| Milk | 140 ml | |
| Salt | 1 pinch | For whipping the egg whites |

## Instructions

{{% steps %}}
1. ## Prepare the Egg Yolk Batter
   In a mixing bowl, whisk together the egg yolks, flour, baking powder, and milk until smooth.

2. ## Whip and Fold Egg Whites
   In a separate bowl, whisk the egg whites with a pinch of salt until stiff peaks form. Add the whipped egg whites to the yolk mixture and fold gently with a spatula without deflating the foam.

3. ## Cook
   Cook in a skillet preheated over medium heat until golden brown on both sides and puffed up.
{{% /steps %}}

## Useful Notes

> [!TIP]
> **Fluffy Pancake Secret**  
> Whipping the egg whites with salt until stiff and folding them gently into the batter without knocking out the air is the secret to achieving that signature extra-thick, fluffy American pancake texture.
