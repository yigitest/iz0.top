# Ninja Strawberry Cheesecake Protein Ice Cream

> [!NOTE]
> **Recipe Summary**  
> A high-protein, low-fat Ninja Creami ice cream prepared with lactose-free protein milk, vanilla protein powder, strawberries, and cheesecake pudding mix.  
> *Nutritional Values: 348 kcal | 43 g Protein | 25 g Carbohydrates | 9 g Fat*

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Lactose-free protein milk (2% fat) | 355 ml | Similar lactose-free or high-protein milk can be used |
| Vanilla protein powder | 1 scoop | - |
| Cheesecake instant pudding mix | 1 tbsp | - |
| Strawberries | 77 g | - |

## Instructions

{{% steps %}}
1. ## Prepare the Mixture
   Add the milk, vanilla protein powder, cheesecake pudding mix, and strawberries to a blender. Blend until completely smooth.

2. ## Freeze
   Pour the mixture into a Ninja Creami pint and secure the lid. Freeze on a level surface until fully frozen.

3. ## Process in Ninja Creami
   Insert the frozen pint into the Ninja Creami and process on the **Lite Ice Cream** program.
{{% /steps %}}
