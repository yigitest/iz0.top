# Chocolate Protein Ice Cream

> [!NOTE]
> **Recipe Summary**  
> A 2-pint chocolate protein ice cream recipe made with whole milk, almond milk, vanilla whey protein, and cocoa powder.  
> *Nutritional Values: Total 575–585 kcal (~290 kcal per pint).*

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Whole milk | 300 ml | - |
| Unsweetened almond milk | 300 ml | - |
| Vanilla whey protein powder | 54 g | - |
| Cocoa powder | 25 g | - |
| Sugar | 20–22 g | - |
| Stabilizer (gum) | Small pinch | Xanthan gum etc. |
| Salt | 1 pinch | - |

## Instructions

{{% steps %}}
1. ## Prepare the Mixture
   Blend or whisk the whole milk, unsweetened almond milk, vanilla whey protein powder, cocoa powder, sugar, stabilizer, and salt until completely smooth.

2. ## Divide into Pints and Freeze
   Divide the mixture evenly between 2 ice cream pints (tubs). Seal with lids and freeze until solid.

3. ## Process in Ninja Creami
   Place the frozen pint into the Ninja Creami and process on the Ice Cream program to serve.
{{% /steps %}}
