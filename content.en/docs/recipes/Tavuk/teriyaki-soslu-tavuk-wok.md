# Teriyaki Chicken Wok Stir-Fry

> [!NOTE]
> **Recipe Summary**  
> A delicious chicken dish marinated in soy sauce, rice vinegar, maple syrup, garlic, and ginger, cooked quickly over high heat in a wok.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Chicken | - | Cut into bite-sized pieces |
| Soy sauce | 1/4 cup | |
| Rice vinegar | 1/4 cup | |
| Maple syrup | 1 tbsp | |
| Ginger | - | Grated or minced |
| Garlic | 4–5 cloves | Minced |
| Black pepper | - | To taste |
| Salt | - | To taste |

## Instructions

{{% steps %}}
1. ## Marinate the Chicken
   In a bowl or sealable container, combine the chicken, soy sauce, rice vinegar, maple syrup, ginger, garlic, black pepper, and salt. Toss until all chicken pieces are thoroughly coated.

2. ## Rest and Stir-Fry over High Heat
   Let the marinated chicken rest in the refrigerator, preferably overnight. When ready to cook, heat a wok until smoking hot and stir-fry the chicken over high heat until browned and cooked through.
{{% /steps %}}

## Useful Notes

> [!TIP]
> **Marination Tip**  
> Letting the chicken marinate overnight in the refrigerator allows it to absorb all the flavors and tenderizes the meat significantly.
