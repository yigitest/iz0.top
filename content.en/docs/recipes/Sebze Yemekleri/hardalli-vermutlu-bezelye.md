# Braised Peas with Mustard and Vermouth

> [!NOTE]
> **Recipe Summary**  
> Sweet green peas gently braised in dry vermouth, olive oil, bay leaves, and sharp Dijon mustard, based on Nigella Lawson's classic recipe. Serves 4.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Green peas | 900 g | Fresh or tender frozen |
| Extra virgin olive oil | 30 ml (2 x 15 ml) | Approx. 2 tbsp |
| Dry vermouth or dry white wine | 125 ml | |
| Salt | 2 tsp | |
| Dijon mustard | 2 tsp | Smooth sharp mustard |
| Bay leaves | 2 leaves | Whole dried |

## Instructions

{{% steps %}}
1. ## Sauté Peas in Olive Oil
   Heat the olive oil (30 ml) in a wide saucepan or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the peas (900 g) and sauté briefly for 2 minutes to coat each pea in oil.

2. ## Deglaze with Vermouth
   Pour in the dry vermouth or white wine (125 ml) and bring to a rapid boil for 1 minute to cook out the alcohol while capturing the aromatic herbal flavor.

3. ## Season and Combine
   Stir in the salt (2 tsp), Dijon mustard (2 tsp), and bay leaves (2). Stir thoroughly so the mustard dissolves evenly into the braising liquids.

4. ## Simmer Covered
   Cover the pan with a tight-fitting lid, lower the heat, and simmer gently for 20 to 30 minutes until the peas are exceptionally tender and deeply infused.

5. ## Rest or Slow Oven Finish (Optional)
   For enhanced flavor melding, either let the peas rest covered off heat for 1 hour, or place the covered pan in a 120°C (250°F) oven for 1 hour before serving warm.
{{% /steps %}}

## Useful Notes

> [!TIP]
> **Vermouth Selection**  
> Dry French white vermouth (such as Noilly Prat) provides botanical notes that balance the sweetness of peas; dry white wine makes an excellent substitute.\n
