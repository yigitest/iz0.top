# Baked Bonito with Vegetables

> [!NOTE]
> **Recipe Summary**  
> Sliced Atlantic bonito baked over a flavorful bed of sliced potatoes and onions, seasoned with tomato sauce, olive oil, lemon, and bay leaves for a tender, aromatic seafood meal.

![Baked Bonito with Vegetables](/images/recipes/firinda-sebzeli-palamut.jpg)

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Bonito | 2 | Sliced |
| Potatoes | 3 medium | Sliced into rounds |
| Onions | 2 | Sliced into rounds |
| Tomatoes | 2 | 1 grated, 1 sliced |
| Lemon | 1 | Sliced |
| Olive oil | 5 tbsp | - |
| Salt | 1 tsp | - |
| Black pepper | 1 tsp | - |
| Bay leaves | 2 | - |

## Instructions

{{% steps %}}
1. ## Preheat the Oven
   Preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F).

2. ## Layer Vegetables and Fish
   Spread the sliced potatoes and onions evenly across the bottom of a baking dish. Arrange the bonito slices on top of the vegetables.

3. ## Prepare and Pour Sauce
   In a bowl, mix together the grated tomato, olive oil, salt, and black pepper. Drizzle the mixture evenly over the ingredients in the baking dish.

4. ## Garnish
   Tuck the tomato slices, lemon slices, and bay leaves in between the fish slices.

5. ## Cover and Bake
   Moisten a sheet of parchment paper with water, wring out excess liquid, and cover the baking dish tightly with it. Bake in the preheated oven for 30 minutes.

6. ## Uncover and Brown
   Remove the parchment paper and continue baking for about 15 minutes, until the tops of the fish and potatoes are nicely browned.

7. ## Serve
   Remove the dish from the oven and serve warm.
{{% /steps %}}
