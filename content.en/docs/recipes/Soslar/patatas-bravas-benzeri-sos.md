# Patatas Bravas Style Dipping Sauce

> [!NOTE]
> **Recipe Summary**  
> A quick, flavorful dipping sauce made with yogurt, mustard, honey, garlic, and olive oil; ideal for roasted potatoes, fries, and roasted vegetables.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Yogurt | 1/2 cup | |
| Mustard | 1 tbsp | |
| Honey | 1 tbsp | |
| Lemon juice | 1/2 lemon | Freshly squeezed |
| Olive oil | 1 tbsp | |
| Garlic | 2–3 cloves | Grated or minced |
| Fresh dill | 1–2 tbsp | Finely chopped (optional) |

## Instructions

{{% steps %}}
1. ## Mix the Sauce
   In a bowl, combine the yogurt, mustard, honey, lemon juice, olive oil, grated garlic, and finely chopped dill (if using). Whisk together until completely smooth and creamy.
{{% /steps %}}

## Useful Notes

> [!TIP]
> **Serving & Tips**  
> Can also be prepared without dill. Pairs wonderfully with roasted cauliflower, french fries, and grilled vegetables.
