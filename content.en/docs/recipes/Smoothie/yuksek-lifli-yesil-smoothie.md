# High-Fiber Green Smoothie

> [!NOTE]
> **Recipe Summary**  
> A nutrient-dense, high-fiber green smoothie prepared with fresh banana, unpeeled pear, frozen avocado, kale, spinach, and ground flaxseed.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Fresh banana | 1 | - |
| Medium pear | 1 | Unpeeled for maximum fiber |
| Frozen avocado | 1/2 cup | - |
| Frozen kale | 1/2 cup | - |
| Frozen spinach | 1/2 cup | - |
| Ground flaxseed | 1 tbsp | Or chia seeds |
| Water or unsweetened plant milk | 1.5 cups | Add an extra 1/4 cup if too thick |

## Instructions

{{% steps %}}
1. ## Prep the Fruit
   Core the pear and cut into chunks.

2. ## Blend
   Add all ingredients into a high-speed blender container. Blend on high until completely smooth.

3. ## Adjust Consistency and Serve
   If the mixture is too thick, add an extra 1/4 cup of liquid and blend again briefly. Enjoy immediately.
{{% /steps %}}
