# Self-Raising Flour

> [!NOTE]
> **Recipe Summary**  
> Quick three-ingredient homemade self-raising flour blend for cakes, biscuits, and quick breads. Yields 1 cup (~130 g).

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| All-purpose flour | 1 cup (~125-130 g) | Standard white flour |
| Baking powder | 1 tsp (~4-5 g) | Fresh leavening agent |
| Salt | 1/2 tsp (~3 g) | Fine table salt |

## Instructions

{{% steps %}}
1. ## Measure and Sift
   Set a fine-mesh sieve over a clean mixing bowl. Add the all-purpose flour (1 cup), baking powder (1 tsp), and fine salt (1/2 tsp).

2. ## Sift and Whisk
   Sift all three ingredients into the bowl. Whisk thoroughly with a wire whisk for 30 seconds to distribute the leavening evenly throughout the flour.

3. ## Use or Store
   Use immediately in place of store-bought self-raising flour, or store in a sealed airtight jar in a cool, dry pantry for up to 3 months.
{{% /steps %}}

## Useful Notes

> [!TIP]
> **Even Leavening**  
> Whisking or sifting thoroughly is essential so baked goods rise evenly without pockets of concentrated baking powder.\n
