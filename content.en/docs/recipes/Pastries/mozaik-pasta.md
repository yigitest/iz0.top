# Classic Turkish Mosaic Cake

> [!NOTE]
> **Recipe Summary**  
> Iconic no-bake Turkish chocolate biscuit cake made with Petit Beurre biscuits, cocoa, melted butter, and milk, shaped into a triangular log and frozen. Yields 11 slices.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Petit Beurre biscuits | 1 kg | Broken into rough pieces |
| Powdered sugar (icing sugar) | 200 g | |
| Unsweetened cocoa powder | 50 g | Rich cocoa |
| Unsalted butter | 250 g | Melted (do not boil) |
| Whole milk | 300 ml | Room temperature |

## Instructions

{{% steps %}}
1. ## Break the Biscuits
   Crush the Petit Beurre biscuits (1 kg) with your hands into rough crumbs in a large, wide mixing bowl. Avoid crushing into fine dust; chunks create the mosaic pattern.

2. ## Add Sugar and Cocoa
   Dust the powdered sugar (200 g) and cocoa powder (50 g) evenly over the broken biscuits.

3. ## Pour Butter and Milk
   Gently melt the butter (250 g) without bringing it to a boil. Pour the warm melted butter and milk (300 ml) directly over the biscuit mixture.

4. ## Knead into Dough
   Begin mixing with your hands or a sturdy spatula. As you knead, the biscuits will soften and bind with the butter and cocoa into a cohesive dough.

5. ## Shape into Triangular Prism
   Turn the mixture onto a large sheet of plastic cling film or a clean plastic bag. Compress firmly and shape with your hands into a triangular prism log.

6. ## Freeze and Slice
   Wrap the log tightly and place in the freezer for 3 to 4 hours until completely firm. Unwrap, slice with a warm sharp knife, and serve chilled.
{{% /steps %}}

## Useful Notes

> [!WARNING]
> **Do Not Add Extra Milk**  
> Do not add extra milk even if the mixture feels dry initially! As you knead, the melted butter distributes and binds the biscuits; excess milk will turn the cake soggy and runny.\n
